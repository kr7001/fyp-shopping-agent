# The claim run, protocol

Written on 20 October 2026 at noon, before the claim market exists. The parts marked
**final** cannot change after this commit. The parts marked **draft** are finalised after the
verifier's logic audit and the dry run, and the final version is committed before the first
paid run. Nothing in this file changes after that.

## The question it answers

Research question 3 on a market the block was not designed on: does the block reach correct
answers while opening fewer listings than exhaustive exploration? And research question 2
again on that market: does the shipped agent fail there as it did on Thornwood?

## What is frozen (final)

Box v9 (form v8, primitives v5, strategy text v7, planner v2, sufficiency rules v4), scorer
v3, openJiuwen at commit 18d45d6 with the recorded local patch (full-index digest
e455d3eb…), @playwright/mcp 0.0.83 with `--allowed-origins http://localhost:7770
--block-service-workers`, Claude Haiku 4.5 through OpenRouter at the agent's own sampling
(temperature 0.2, top_p 0.1), 100 steps, 600 seconds. If a fault is found in any of these
after the claim market is generated, it is recorded, fixed, and the claim is re-run on
another fresh market generated by the same rule. The fix is never tested on this one.

## The market (final)

- **Generator.** `env/generate_apparel.py` with its 13 acceptance rules unchanged, four sizes
  (S, M, L, XL), 15 listings, the same price model as Thornwood.
- **Page rule.** `env/pick_apparel_market.py`'s rule, unchanged in what it states: the answer
  renders outside the first four cards, and a listing inside the first four advertises less
  than the answer costs. Its implementation is corrected (20 October, before any seed was
  generated) to read the price the card advertises, the lowest buyable size, where the code
  had taken the lowest of all sizes including sold-out ones.
- **Seed rule.** Seeds 204, 205, 206 and on, in that order (Thornwood was 203). The claim
  market is the first seed that passes the 13 rules and the page rule. No candidate is
  rejected by hand. Every seed tried is recorded with its outcome.
- **Brand rule.** The first of Ashcombe, Hartwell, Wyndham that the shop's search returns no
  product for before seeding, and that is no word of any seller name the generator can draw
  (amended 20 October, before any seed was generated: Kelsall was removed because it is a
  seller first name in the generator's pool, and the scorer matches a seller by its first
  word, so a Kelsall brand would score every answer as naming a Kelsall seller).
- **Thornwood stays.** The claim market uses its own SKU prefix and URL keys, so Thornwood's
  15 listings, its fixture and its task files are untouched, and the development batches
  stay reproducible.
- **Checked in the live shop before any run.** The results page for the brand lists exactly
  the 15 listings with no next page. Every card's advertised price equals the fixture's
  lowest buyable price for that listing (the bound the block relies on). Every size's label,
  price and salability equals the fixture's. The gated theme shows only the advertised price
  until a size is chosen. Thornwood's results page is unchanged.

## Questions (draft)

The six questions the Thornwood cells asked (the results-page control and the five gated
questions), worded identically with the new brand. Keys are computed from the new fixture by
code, and the buyable reading of the two "offer" questions is computed the same way. Whether
the newer question shapes join the claim run or follow it is decided at 7 pm on 20 October.

## Arms (draft)

1. **Shipped:** openJiuwen as shipped, as cell 1.
2. **Open everything:** as shipped, with cell 5's instruction to open every listing, word for
   word.
3. **The block:** box v9, answering its own question about "offer" with the reading the key
   was written under (menu). The two "offer" questions also run under the other reading
   (buyable), as a separate invocation.

Every arm runs with the block's answer check off. The check takes no browser action, so
replaying it from the logs afterwards is equivalent to running it, and it gives both the raw
and the checked figure from the same runs.

## Order (draft)

Interleaved by repeat: repeat 1 of shipped, open everything and the block, then repeat 2,
then repeat 3. Any drift in the model endpoint over the evening then falls on every arm
alike.

## Measures (draft)

- Primary: correct under the key fixed before the run. Listings opened (the interaction
  count). Cost per run (the provider's charge).
- Secondary: opened what the answer needs (the floors `verify_cells` computes from the
  fixture). Browser actions. Asks on the clear questions and on the ambiguous ones. The
  block's check, replayed: refusals of wrong answers and of correct ones.

## Analysis (draft)

Per question first, then pooled. Two-sided exact tests within question
(`compare_cells.stratified_exact`): the block against shipped, and the block against open
everything, on correct and on opened what it needs. Fifteen runs per arm cannot show that
two arms are equal, so "comparable" is reported as counts, never as a test result.

## Exclusions (draft)

A run with a silence over 60 seconds (the laptop slept) is void and re-run once at the end.
A run that hits the step budget or gives no answer counts as not correct. Nothing else is
excluded.

## Limits stated in advance (final)

- The question templates are in sample: the rules were written on them. The market is not.
- One model and one scaffold. The second agent cannot run behind the block until its browser
  is confined to the shop.
- Markets are generated so that a card-only answer is wrong. The claim is conditional on
  gating being present. How often it is present in real shops is research question 1,
  answered separately.
