Main findings

Six results, in the order they were found
#FindingEvidenceConsequence
1Structured data does not publish the price rangeBoth the live marketplace and WebArena publish a single price where the standard defines a low-to-high rangeA shortcut that would let the agent skip listings does not exist. Prices must be read from the page
2WebArena cannot show this failure as shippedAll 104,368 products are single-price. Options exist but change nothingEnvironment extension became a required first step, not an optional extra
3The agent never signals uncertainty0 abstentions in 97 unmodified runsAnswers this project's RQ2. A caller cannot tell a guess from a verified answer
4Only under-retrieval predicts failureWrong answers cluster at few actions. Past a threshold, extra looking buys nothing measurableThere is a minimum budget below which answers stop being reliable. That is the RQ3 premise, measured
5The failure needs scale to appearWith two named listings the agent scored 5 of 5. With fifteen it failsA negative result that redirected the work. The trap is about cost, not about trickery
6Right and earned are different propertiesOne question is answered after opening exactly 4 of 15 listings in all seven runs, right and wrong alikeIts one correct answer rests on identical evidence to its six wrong ones. Accuracy alone cannot see this

FINDING 3, AS IT ACTUALLY APPEARED

Q. Does every Rivenhall listing stock the 2 x 9.5L pack? A. Yes Wrong. Five of fifteen do not stock it. The agent had opened four listings. No hedge, no qualification, three separate times.

THE SAME QUESTION, AFTER THE FIX

A. No. Out of 15 total listings, only 10 stock this pack size. The 5 that do not are: Chandler Wholesale, Pembroke Trading, ... Correct, with its evidence, three times out of three. The fix does not tell it the answer. It tells it what was missing.

Main challenges

What went wrong, and what it cost
ChallengeWhat happenedResolution
Cheap models could not do the taskThe proposal assumed a cheap model tier. One could not follow the required output format; others were blocked by their providers regardless of creditEverything runs on a model that works and is not free. This is the direct cause of the budget problem
Silent environment failureThe shop reported itself healthy while its search engine was hung, so the catalogue looked empty for no visible reasonDiagnosed and documented. Two wrong theories were tried first and are recorded as such
Two bugs inside the agentA valid empty result was reported as a parse error, and a tool failure did not reach the caller, which reported successBoth patched locally. Candidates for contributing back, since the agent is open source
I misdiagnosed a failure as fabricationReported that the agent invented prices. It had not. $15.01 is a real surcharge on a $9.89 base, and 9.89 + 15.01 = 24.90, the true price. It misread the meaning of a number it had correctly readCorrected in every document. The distinction matters: a misreading is fixable by a rule, an invention is not
My marking rules were wrong twiceOne rule penalised an answer for mentioning the alternatives, which is what a good comparison does. Another penalised an answer for showing its own arithmeticBoth fixed and all stored runs re-marked at no cost. One published claim had to be withdrawn as a result
My own safety check blocked a correct answer"$17.68, Redhill Stores" is right, but $17.68 never appears on the page, being a $6.82 base plus a $10.86 surcharge. The check only trusted answers that spelled out the sumFixed, which cost some detection power, because two of its catches turned out to be accidental. Losing an accidental catch is the correct trade
Results scored against a moved answer keyThe catalogue is regenerated between rounds, so older runs were being marked against prices that no longer appliedDetected automatically by re-marking and checking for disagreement. Four runs excluded. Without this, the method looked worse than it is

Five of these seven are mistakes I made rather than problems I encountered. They are listed because each one changed a number that had already been written down, and the record of catching them is part of what the work is.
