Summary
Set up the WebArena shopping environment locally and audited how product pricing is exposed to agents. Most of the week went into environment troubleshooting. The audit produced one finding that changes the method.
________________


1. Environment
WebArena shopping site now running locally via Docker, using the ServiceNow WebArena-Verified distribution rather than the original, as its images are considerably smaller and it corrects known annotation bugs.
Setup was not straightforward. The image depends on Elasticsearch, which hangs during startup on Apple Silicon. Because the process still reports as running, the failure is silent, and it leaves the Magento indexes unbuilt so the catalogue appears almost empty. Resolved by a full container restart and a manual reindex. Setup procedure and timings are documented for reproducibility.
________________


2. Finding: catalogue composition
The catalogue holds 104,368 products, all of type simple. There are no configurable products.
Product options do exist, but they are Magento custom options, meaning one price regardless of selection. For example a cake topper offers ten colours, all at $9.49.
Consequence. The environment provides interaction-gated options but not interaction-gated pricing. Configurable products with divergent per-variant prices will be authored for the experiments. This is workable and gives cleaner ground truth, since which products are mine will be documented explicitly.
________________


3. Finding: structured data collapses configurations to one price
Checked what machine-readable product data each platform publishes.
  

Schema.org defines AggregateOffer with lowPrice and highPrice for exactly the case of a product available at multiple prices. Neither platform uses it. Both present a set of purchasable configurations as a single number.
An agent trusting structured data over the rendered page, which is the standard approach, receives a confidently wrong answer with no ambiguity marker.
Consequence for the method. The proposal's branch-and-bound approach for "which variant is cheapest" assumed a published lower bound to prune listings without opening them. That bound is not available from structured data on either platform. It must be parsed from the rendered listing instead. Discovered before implementation rather than after, and will be stated as a limitation.
